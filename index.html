Getting ready

A pre-listing checklist.

Small things move the needle in a market where buyers scroll through listings on their phones first.

  • Choose a local agentInterview more than one. Ask how they price, where they market, and what happened with their last few listings.
  • Fix the small stuffPaint touch-ups, lightbulbs, a clean deck and a tidy gear room make a big difference in photos and in person.
  • Professional photos and droneIn Squamish the view is part of the price. Aerials show buyers where the home sits on the mountain.
  • Gather your documentsStrata minutes, permits, renovation receipts and utility costs help buyers commit faster.
  • Time it with the lightLong summer evenings and clear fall days photograph best. Plan your shoot around the weather.
